Our Story

A Dream for Inclusive Education

In 2006, William and Lorraine Mbu envisioned a school where all children receive a quality education in an inclusive environment — a place where no child is left behind due to disability or limited resources.

Today, Ss. Mary and Elizabeth Nursery and Primary Academy stands as a testament to that vision, serving hundreds of students in the Buea community with well-trained faculty and modern facilities.

  • Inclusive education for children with special needs
  • Well-trained, well-paid local faculty
  • Modern 3-story facility with 20 classrooms
  • Transportation services for all students
