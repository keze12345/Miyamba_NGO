Our Story

A Dream Born
in Buea, 2006

William and Lorraine Mbu envisioned a school where every child — regardless of disability, background, or circumstance — could access world-class education.

Today, that vision stands three stories tall in Buea. Ss. Mary & Elizabeth Academy has become a beacon of inclusive education in Cameroon, with university-trained faculty, modern classrooms, and a community built on the belief that learning is a right, not a privilege.

100% Inclusive

Every student welcomed, including those with special needs

Expert Faculty

Recruited from the University of Buea, well-trained & well-paid

Modern Campus

3-story building, 20 classrooms, dedicated support spaces

The first visible work on the Buea site marked the move from a hopeful idea into a real place where children could one day learn.

Foundations Take Shape

Construction advanced through careful foundation work, turning open land into the footprint of a future academy.

Building phase

The structure grew floor by floor, with classroom space planned for a school that could serve more children over time.

Expansion
